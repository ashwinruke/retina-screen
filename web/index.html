Diabetic retinopathy screening

Grades a colour fundus photo from 0 (no retinopathy) to 4 (proliferative) and shows which regions of the retina drove the result. The model runs in your browser, so your photo is never uploaded.

The needle is the model's severity score. Coloured bands show where each grade starts.

About the model

EfficientNet-B3 trained on APTOS 2019 fundus photos.

Photos are cropped to the retina, resized to 512 pixels and colour-normalised before grading. The heatmap is Grad-CAM: warmer areas raised the severity score more.

Code, training and evaluation on GitHub

Not a medical device

This is a research demo. It has not been clinically validated and must not be used to diagnose or rule out disease. It grades diabetic retinopathy only and does not detect other eye conditions or judge whether a photo is good enough to grade.
